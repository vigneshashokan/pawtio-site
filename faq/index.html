The basics

What is Pawtio?

A calm, chronic-care-first pet health tracker for iPhone. Record your vet visit and get a clear summary of what was said, keep meds, labs, vaccines and weight in one place, and walk into the next visit prepared.

How much does it cost?

Pawtio is free to download on the App Store.

Which devices does it work on?

Pawtio is made for iPhone and needs iOS 16.4 or later.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Pawtio is iPhone-only for now. [Add your Android plans or a waitlist link here.]

Can I add more than one pet?

Yes. Every pet gets its own profile and records, and you can switch between them in a tap.

Recording & summaries

Should I tell my vet I'm recording?

Yes, always ask first. Pawtio reminds you on the recording screen too. Most vets are glad you want to remember the details, and the rules about recording conversations vary from place to place.

Does recording work with my phone locked?

Yes. Recording keeps going with your phone locked, and a Live Activity shows it's running. On iPhones with the Dynamic Island, long-press it to pause, resume or stop.

What if the summary gets something wrong?

Every field shows how confident Pawtio is, and you can tap any item to correct it.

Can I add records I didn't record?

Yes. Add anything by hand, or scan a lab report, prescription or vaccine certificate and let Pawtio fill in the details.

Privacy & your data

Where does my audio go?

Nowhere. Recordings are transcribed on your iPhone and never uploaded. Once a summary is written, you choose whether to keep or delete each recording.

Who processes my summaries and scans?

Summaries, scans and Ask Pawtio answers are processed by a server on a paid AI tier that does not use your data to train models.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Export all of your data whenever you like, or delete your account and everything in it from Settings. You can also delete your account on the web.

Do you show ads or sell data?

No ads, no data brokers. Your pet's records belong to you.

Care & safety

Does Pawtio give medical advice?

No. Pawtio is not a diagnostic tool and does not tell you what is wrong with your pet. It organizes what your vet says, and your vet makes the calls. If your pet is unwell, call your vet.

Can my partner or pet sitter use it too?

Yes. Invite them to your care circle and they can help with every pet you share. When someone gives a dose, everyone sees it and who gave it.

Does Pawtio work offline?

Yes. You can view and edit records without a connection.
