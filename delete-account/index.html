Delete your Pawtio account

For Pawtio: Pet Health Records, on Android and iOS

In the app

Open Settings, tap your account at the top, then tap Delete account & all data and confirm. Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email support@pawtio.app from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Pawtio account". We'll confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, name and sign-in details.
  • Every pet you own, and everything recorded for them: visits and their summaries, medications, labs, vaccines, weights, appointments, notes and questions.
  • Uploaded files and photos: lab files, scanned documents, pet and profile photos.
  • Caregivers you invited lose access to your pets.

What is kept

  • Pets that someone else owns and shared with you stay with their owner. You lose access to them.
  • Visit recordings were never on our servers. They stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • We keep nothing else once your account is deleted.
