About Pawtio

Built for the pets who need a little more care.

What we believe

The promises Pawtio is built around.

Calm by design

Plain language and a quiet interface. Looking after a sick pet is stressful enough without an app adding to it.

Your vet makes the calls

Pawtio organizes what your vet says. It isn't a diagnostic tool, and it never gives medical advice.

Private by default

Audio is transcribed on your phone and never uploaded. No ads, no data brokers, and no training on your data.

Your records are yours

Export everything whenever you like, or delete your account and everything in it from Settings.
