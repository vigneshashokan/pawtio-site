Features & guide

Everything Pawtio does, and how to get the most from it.

Pawtio is built around the vet visit and everything that happens between visits. Here's each part, with practical tips for using it well.

01 · Record the visit

Just hit record.

Tap record when the consult begins. Recording keeps going with your phone locked, and a Live Activity shows it's running. In the Dynamic Island you'll see your pet, the timer and live sound levels; long-press it to pause, resume or stop.

Get the most out of it

  • Tell your vet you're recording before you start. Most are glad you want to get the details right.
  • Start when the consult begins, not at the end. The key details often come up during the exam.
  • Add your questions to Prep & questions beforehand, so they're in front of you in the room.
  • Set your phone face up near the conversation rather than in a pocket or bag.

02 · The summary

See what the vet said.

Pawtio transcribes the conversation on your device and turns it into a structured summary: what was discussed, what changed and what to watch. Every field shows how confident it is, and you can correct anything that came out wrong.

Get the most out of it

  • Review the summary the same day, while the visit is still fresh.
  • Start with any low-confidence fields. A quick tap fixes a misheard dose or date.
  • Send new medications to your meds list, follow-ups to your next brief, and anything to watch to your evening check-in.

03 · History & Ask Pawtio

Every visit, on file.

Visits, medications and prescription diets, lab results, vaccinations, journal notes and photos sit in one searchable timeline. Add records by hand, or scan a lab report, prescription or vaccine certificate and let Pawtio fill in the details. Then ask Pawtio about your pet's records in plain language.

Get the most out of it

  • Scan paperwork as soon as you're home, while it's still in your bag.
  • Use the journal for small things, like a limp or an off day. Over time they become a pattern your vet can use.
  • Before an appointment, ask "What's changed since the last visit?"
  • More than one pet? Each gets its own profile and records, and you switch between them in a tap.

04 · Care circle

Care as a team.

Invite a partner, family member or sitter to your care circle, and they can help with every pet you share. When someone gives a dose, everyone sees it and who gave it, and you can send a caregiver a reminder when a dose is due.

Get the most out of it

  • Log a dose the moment you give it. That's how everyone else knows not to double up.
  • Going away? Add your sitter before you leave and send them the emergency snapshot.
  • Send a dose reminder from Pawtio instead of a text message.

05 · Chronic care

Built for the long haul.

Kidney disease, diabetes, arthritis, heart conditions: whatever your pet is managing over months and years. Pawtio covers dose reminders, vaccinations due, condition trackers, a quick evening check-in and injection-site rotation. It keeps the thread, so you aren't rebuilding the story from memory at every visit.

Get the most out of it

  • Do the evening check-in daily. It's a few quick questions, and over weeks it shows the trend.
  • Watch for "Running low" on a medication, so a refill never turns into a scramble.
  • If your pet has regular injections, use injection-site rotation to keep track of the sites.
  • Sync appointments to your calendar so the next visit, and its brief, never sneak up on you.

06 · Visit prep

Walk in ready.

Before each appointment, Pawtio builds a brief from what has happened since the last visit: weight trend, active medications and questions worth asking. Share it as a PDF with your vet or a specialist.

Get the most out of it

  • Open the brief the night before and add anything else you want to ask.
  • Share the PDF with the clinic ahead of time, so your vet walks in up to speed.
  • Seeing a specialist or moving clinics? Send the records pack instead of chasing old files.

Privacy

Private by default.

Your pet's records belong to you. Here's exactly how Pawtio treats them.

Read the privacy policy
  • Audio is transcribed on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • You choose whether to keep or delete each recording once its summary is written.
  • Summaries, scans and Ask Pawtio answers are processed on a paid AI tier that does not use your data to train models.
  • Export everything whenever you like, or delete your account and all of it from Settings.
  • No ads. No data brokers.
