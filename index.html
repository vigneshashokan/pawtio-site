Private by default

Recordings are transcribed on your device and never uploaded. Once a summary is written, you choose whether to keep or delete each one.

Questions, answered.

The short version of what people ask most.

See all questions

Is Pawtio free?

Yes. Pawtio is free to download on the App Store for iPhone.

Should I tell my vet I'm recording?

Yes, always ask first. Pawtio reminds you on the recording screen, and most vets are glad you want to remember the details.

Does Pawtio give medical advice?

No. Pawtio organizes what your vet says, and your vet makes the calls. If your pet is unwell, call your vet.

Can my family help with care?

Yes. Invite a partner, family member or sitter to your care circle, and they can help with every pet you share.
